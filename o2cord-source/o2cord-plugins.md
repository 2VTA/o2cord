# o2cord plugins

The public o2cord plugins: the ones everyone with o2cord has. Where to find them in Discord: Settings → o2cord (the "o2cord plugins" list, each with its settings) and Settings → Plugins → the "o2cord" filter.

The list lives in one place, `src/utils/o2PluginList.ts` - the Plugins tab and the o2cord page read it from there. When you change it, update the table below to match.

| # | Plugin | What it does |
|---|--------|--------------|
| 1 | **o2Settings** | Quick switches and the theme choice (Normal, Retro Terminal, VHS) |
| 2 | **O2WhatsNew** | Shows what's new in o2cord after each update |
| 3 | **ProfileTheme** | A picture behind a whole profile card (big window, small card and the side panel) |
| 4 | **ProfileImages** | Profile pictures added by the o2cord team, with adjustable brightness |
| 5 | **CustomProfile** | Change how your own profile looks: name, picture, banner, badges, bio, effects and frames |
| 6 | **Nameplate** | A background behind usernames in the member list |
| 7 | **ProfileSong** | A short song plays when you open the profile of someone who has one |
| 8 | **CustomTyping** | Shows other o2cord users' own typing phrase instead of "is typing..." |
| 9 | **ussro2Public** | Shared o2 profile backgrounds, shown for everyone on this build |
| 10 | **CallieRequests** | Send picture, theme, background and typing-phrase requests to Callie |
| 11 | **RetroTerminal** | Discord as an old CRT screen (theme) |
| 12 | **VHS** | Discord as an old VHS tape (theme) |
| 13 | **O2Voice** | Voice changer for voice channels (needs the free VB-CABLE) |
| 14 | **O2Presence** | Your own Rich Presence: presets, live preview, rotation and buttons |
| 15 | **FakePlaying** | Shows "Playing X" on your profile |
| 16 | **AntiMoveDeco** | A button that stops you from being moved or disconnected in a voice channel |
| 17 | **NitroServer** | Every server at max Boost tier, custom role icons and server banner (cosmetic) |
| 18 | **AppIcon** | Change the picture on Discord's window and taskbar button |
| 19 | **O2WindowControls** | Your own images on the window buttons |
| 20 | **PluginShare** | Send a plugin to friends: pick them in a Forward-style window, they get a card with its switch right in the message |
| 21 | **Widget** | A cosmetic two-card widget on your profile settings |

## Themes

Chosen on the o2cord page (Theme cards):

- **Normal** - Discord's own look.
- **Retro Terminal** - the RetroTerminal plugin above.
- **VHS** - the VHS plugin above.
